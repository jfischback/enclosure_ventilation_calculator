CriteriaThe 5% NDO and 200 fpm face velocity criteria come from EPA Method 204 (40 CFR Part 51, Appendix M).

Pressure differenceMethod 204 treats a pressure drop of 0.007 in. w.c. across the enclosure as equivalent to 200 fpm face velocity, so either check can show adequate inward flow.

Transport velocity2,000 fpm is for vapors and gases; particulate typically needs 3,500–4,500 fpm (ACGIH Industrial Ventilation manual).
